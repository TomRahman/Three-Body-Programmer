Three Body Problem Simulation

Three "planets" pulling on each other with gravity, no walls, so they just float freely like they would in space. I started from a Python/pygame version that bounced bodies off the edges of the screen, but that isn't really the three body problem (there aren't any walls in space!) so I rewrote it in JavaScript and took the walls out. Click anywhere to add another body. Sometimes two bodies swing close and one gets flung off the screen - that isn't a bug, it's the three body problem being chaotic (a close pass acts like a gravitational slingshot). Press Restart to start it over.
